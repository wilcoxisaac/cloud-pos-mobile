# Cloud POS — Version 2.0.0 Release Notes

**Release Date:** March 21, 2026
**Version:** 2.0.0 (Build 2)
**Platform:** iOS & Android

---

## What's New in Version 2

### Customer Repository & Loyalty Program
Introducing a dedicated Customers tab — a full customer relationship database synchronized with Cloud POS backoffice.

- View all customers with loyalty tier badges: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum
- Tier progress bar shows points needed to reach the next tier (Bronze: 0–499 pts, Silver: 500–1,999 pts, Gold: 2,000–4,999 pts, Platinum: 5,000+ pts)
- Customer detail profile shows total spend, visit count, last visit date, top purchased items, and complete quote/invoice/order history
- One-tap sync pulls all customers from Cloud POS and merges loyalty points and visit counts automatically
- Filter customers by tier; sort by total spend
- Customer auto-fill in quote and invoice creation — pick an existing customer to pre-populate name, email, and phone

### Customer Portal
Customers can now receive a secure, personalized link to review and act on their quotes and invoices from any browser — no login required.

- Customers can review itemized quotes and tap Accept or Decline
- Accepted quotes automatically convert to invoices and trigger an invoice email
- Customers can pay invoices online by card directly from the portal
- Loyalty points are awarded automatically (1 point per dollar) upon portal payment

### Automated Email Workflows
- Quotes are emailed to customers instantly when created, with a link to their portal
- Invoices are emailed when created or when a quote is accepted, with a secure payment link
- All emails sent from a branded address with itemized details

### Contactless Payments (NFC)
- Accept contactless card payments using iPhone NFC (Tap to Pay on iPhone)
- Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, and UnionPay supported
- Simulation mode available for testing without hardware

### Product Catalog Browser
- Browse the full product catalog from within the quote and invoice creation flow
- Filter by category; tap any item to add it instantly to the document
- Returns automatically to the document after adding an item
